Report defects and feature requests in the

Professional support is available from

## Credits

The original extension was initiated by civiuser (Sidney) and Pierre Morvan:

Version 1 was published by Makoa and developed by Antoine Breheret and Dewy

## Support the project

The extension is independently developed and currently has no institutional or
corporate sponsor. Organizations using this work can help fund maintenance,
documentation, testing and future development.

To sponsor work on HelloAsso Payment Processor or request professional support,
[contact Jonathan Dahan](https://www.jonathan.dhn.one/).
